Belgian athletes — scoring breakdown

Every World Triathlon ranking counts an athlete's best 6 results from the last ~52 weeks ("Current") plus their best 6 from the 52 weeks before that ("Previous") — 12 results max. Click an athlete to see every race and the exact score they'd need to beat to bump a result out. A period with fewer than 6 used has no threshold yet — any new counted result adds straight to their total.

Roster scope: Belgian athletes currently ranked in the World Top 400 (Men/Women separately) who have raced at least once in the last 12 months. Athletes who drop out of the Top 400 or go 12+ months without a race are treated as inactive/retired and removed automatically each week — no manual list to maintain.

Athlete deep dive

Pick a Belgian athlete to see, for every upcoming competition on the calendar right now, an estimate of how they'd likely score there — and right next to it, the exact score they'd need to beat in the relevant World Ranking scoring period to actually improve their total. Same underlying model as Opportunity Scoring, just organized around one athlete at a time.

Upcoming Elite start lists

World Triathlon opens the initial start list ~32 days before an event, allows National Federation substitutions until ~30 days out, and finalizes invitations ~28 days out. This scans every upcoming Elite Men / Elite Women program currently listed and flags any Belgian entries. An event showing "Field: 0" hasn't had its start list published yet — check back as it gets closer.
